Family 1: sixteen groups of four

One position becomes four vertices

A Clebsch graph has 16 vertices. Here each Clebsch position is replaced by four vertices. Every family uses the same internal coloring.

The sixteen positions are shown as a grid for readability. Their location on the screen does not define adjacency: the binary labels do.

A square records connections, not another family

Rows and columns list the same twelve families. Select a square: it contains all 64 × 64 entries between its row family and column family. The twelve diagonal squares are the interiors of the families.

These controls change probabilities for whole groups of connections. They do not recolor the published graph. Even at p = ¾ and h = ½, this recipe differs from its raw density summary.

All 768 vertices, ordered by family

Column = destination family · row = source family. Symmetry mirrors every connection across the diagonal.
