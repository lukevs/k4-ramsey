## Mathematical certificate and domain coverage

Write p_i for the probability that an independently sampled triple has i red
edges, and J_ij for the joint event on two disjoint triples. For every fixed
probability graphon, J_ij=p_i*p_j and sum_i p_i=1. These are unrestricted
sampling identities; they do not assume a finite construction or regularity.
On a probability box a_i<=p_i<=b_i, the added inequalities are:

- (a_i+b_i)p_i-J_ii-a_i*b_i >= 0;
- J_ij-a_i*p_j-a_j*p_i+a_i*a_j >= 0;
- J_ij-b_i*p_j-b_j*p_i+b_i*b_j >= 0;
- b_i*p_j+a_j*p_i-J_ij-b_i*a_j >= 0;
- a_i*p_j+b_j*p_i-J_ij-a_i*b_j >= 0.

Under factorization, each is the product of two nonnegative distances to box
endpoints. These are ordinary secant and McCormick bounds, not a claim of newly
invented inequalities. The 2026 physics paper motivated conditioning and
observable selection; the actual gains here come from these independence cuts.

Only p1 and p2 are subdivided. Within each box, 0<=p0,p3<=1-a1-a2 follows
from nonnegativity and sum p_i=1. Constraints for all four patterns, including
mixed pairs, are present. The programme does not impose p1=p2. Instead, colour
complementation swaps p1 and p2 while preserving the objective, so it suffices
to cover p1<=p2 and apply the result to a graphon or its complement.

If either coordinate is outside [5/16,7/16], the existing four outer N6 cases
apply, with colour complement when needed. Within the central square, the
combination checker forms the exact arrangement of every rectangle boundary.
It checks all open cells, boundary segments and vertices, restricted to p1<=p2,
using rational midpoints/endpoints. For each cell it takes the strongest checked
certificate covering it; then takes the minimum over cells and outer cases.
Retaining parent certificates ensures a numerically poorer child cannot lower
the final certified result. No favourable-only subset of cases is discarded.

For each graph H, the certificate checks

    c(H) - sum_b <Q_b,C_b(H)> - sum_k lambda_k*g_k(H) >= L,

with rational Q_b positive definite and lambda_k>=0. Averaging over a graphon
inside the specified box proves F(W)>=L. Scalar constants are incorporated in
each g_k coefficient using the normalization sum_H y_H=1.

## Verification and reproducibility

The 58 N7/N6 Gram tensors are unchanged and bound by SHA256 to the earlier
independent exhaustive enumeration. The new four marginal and sixteen joint
triple rows were generated by unordered disjoint triples, then independently
recounted by a C++ checker using all 5040 vertex permutations on all 1044 graphs.
An additional rational sanity check verifies the product identities on actual
independent pattern probability vectors, including boundaries.

`check.py` imports neither the solver, scalar-row generator nor certificate
proposer. It rebuilds scalar constraints with Fraction arithmetic, verifies
all 58 rational matrices by exact LDL, independently accumulates graph-wise
integer penalties, and checks the bound. The combined certificate additionally
checks exact coverage and artifact hashes. This is an exact computer-assisted
argument plus the flag-square proof; it is not a Lean/kernel theorem.

Paths: experiments/energy_bootstrap_joint/ and reports/energy-bootstrap-joint-001/.

NAME-certificate.json, NAME-check.json and logs. Combined-N-check.json files

Use the existing offline uv environment with numpy, scipy, cvxpy and networkx,
