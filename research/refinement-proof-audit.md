# Typed-refinement and recursion proof audit

Date: 2026-09-27.  This reviews H-ROOT-001/002, the one-level
`latent-precision-002` construction, the relationship with nested profile
composition, and the proposed Seidel-switching obstruction.  It distinguishes
three claims that should not be conflated:

1. a finite typed refinement is a valid step graphon;
2. a sequence of finite typed refinements has convergent `K4` density;
3. the sequence represents one measurable infinite recursive graphon.

The first claim is enough for the current improved candidate.  The second is
enough for an asymptotic upper bound from a recursive family.  The third needs
additional consistency, but is useful when claiming a self-similar graphon.

## 1. The 384-class latent refinement is already valid

`reports/latent-precision-002/graphon-candidate.json` is an ordinary rational
step graphon with

    r = 384, q = 65536, equal class masses,
    A_ij in {0, 30263, 39767, 46312, 55816, 65536},
    A_ii = 0.

It is obtained by splitting every old class into two equal latent signs and,
on the supported fractional blocks, using

    P'((i,s),(j,t)) = P_ij + epsilon s t,
    epsilon = -297/4096 = -4752/65536.

This preserves every old coarse-block mean.  It is not necessary to recurse
the split to justify the result: apply the rational-step first-moment theorem
from `graphon-realization-proof.md` directly with 384 classes.  The independent
ordered recount reports

    c4 <= 515776822961911272515012376329
          / 17113283103081096920205493272576
       ~= 0.030138975663240804.

The raw normalization is exactly

    384^4 * 65536^6
      = 1722679482537250971283333950123326570496.

All ordered microclass quadruples are included, including repeated base and
microclass labels.  In the finite realization, the four sampled vertices are
conditioned to be distinct and their six vertex edges are independently
randomized.  Thus the repeated-index and collision analysis is unchanged.

The evidence boundary is also unchanged: the selected cubic/quartic expansion
agrees with an independent exact ordered-index C++ recount using a wide outer
accumulator and tiny literal tests.  It is not yet a compiled-Lean recount or
a kernel theorem.  No recursive claim is needed to promote it within this
evidence class.

## 2. Two mathematically sufficient models of recursion

### 2.1 Nested graph composition and finite profile transfer

Even-Zohar and Linial's [composition framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.1205)
uses repetitive profiles, in which vertices are sampled with replacement.
For a fixed outer graph `G`, composition with a child graph induces a linear
stochastic operator on the child's labeled `t`-profile.  At `t=4` there are
64 labeled graph states and 11 unlabeled isomorphism states.  For their fixed
core, the nested four-profile is the Perron--Frobenius stationary vector of the
11-state operator.  Their partition formula explicitly includes repeated
outer labels; ordinary distinct-vertex profiles have the same limit because
vertex collisions vanish.

The same organization can cover a rational stochastic outer kernel.  Fix an
outer class label for each sampled vertex, partition the four sample positions
by equal labels, multiply the rational probabilities of all cross-part edges,
and use the child's repetitive profile inside each part.  Summing over ignored
child edges makes this a nonnegative linear operator on the complete labeled
four-profile.  Its columns (or rows, depending on convention) sum to one.
Permutation invariance permits reduction to the 11 unlabeled states.

This applies directly only to a **single shared child object inserted into
every outer class**, with cross-class probabilities fixed by the outer kernel.
If children have several persistent types, one must enlarge the state to mixed
typed profiles (graph isomorphism state plus the type labels/partition of the
four roots).  It need not remain an 11-state system.  A finite transfer system
still exists for a genuinely finite-state substitution rule, but the operator
must be derived; treating each type's 11-vector independently generally misses
mixed samples.

For a finite stochastic matrix `M`, finding a stationary vector `Mv=v` is not
by itself a convergence proof.  A convenient sufficient check is that the
relevant stochastic matrix is primitive; for an irreducible chain this is the
same as irreducibility plus aperiodicity.  More generally, one may check the
cyclic subspace generated by the actual initial profile: every eigenvalue that
appears there other than 1 must have modulus strictly less than 1, with no
nontrivial Jordan behavior at 1.  Simplicity of eigenvalue 1 gives a unique
initial-independent stationary limit, but is stronger than some
initial-specific convergence statements.  A reducible or periodic operator
can have a stationary vector while `M^d v0` depends on `v0` or oscillates.  The
recursive claim therefore needs:

1. an exact transfer operator with nonnegative entries and correct stochastic
   normalization;
2. proof that the finite-depth profile really satisfies `v_(d+1)=M v_d`;
3. a primitivity/spectral or direct contraction proof on the reachable state;
4. evaluation of `K4 + anti-K4` as the correct linear functional of the
   limiting profile.

If only that objective is needed, it is enough to prove that the corresponding
linear functional of `M^d v0` converges; full profile convergence is stronger
than necessary.

### 2.2 Mean-preserving graphon refinement

A different recursion refines off-diagonal probability cells themselves.
Put every finite refinement on one product probability space and let
`F_d` be the sigma-algebra generated by the first `d` vertex-type coordinates.
If symmetric step graphons `W_d : Omega^2 -> [0,1]` satisfy

    E[W_(d+1) | F_d tensor F_d] = W_d,

then `(W_d)` is a bounded martingale on `Omega^2`.  It converges almost
everywhere and in `L1` to a symmetric measurable graphon `W_infinity`.
For graphons in `[0,1]`, telescoping the six edge factors gives

    |t(K4,U)-t(K4,W)| <= 6 ||U-W||_1,

and applying the same inequality to complements gives

    |mono4(U)-mono4(W)| <= 12 ||U-W||_1.

Hence the monochromatic density of the finite refinements converges to that
of `W_infinity`.  Zero mean over each parent pair-cell is the condition needed
for this martingale; zero row sums are stronger and preserve additional rooted
statistics.

Mean preservation is not the only possible proof.  Summable `L1` increments
or a direct Cauchy estimate also suffice.  But “every level has finitely many
types” alone implies neither pointwise nor density convergence.

## 3. A canonical infinite-word graphon for ordinary composition

For standard nested composition, take an infinite word of outer labels for
each graphon point.  For two words, use the outer edge rule at their first
coordinate of disagreement; the value on identical infinite words can be set
arbitrarily.  With 192 equal outer labels, the probability that two independent
words agree through depth `d` is `192^(-d)`, so the first disagreement is
finite almost surely.  The cylinder approximations are measurable and converge
in `L1`; changing the unresolved depth-`d` diagonal cells changes area at most
`192^(-d)`.  Consequently the monochromatic `K4` density error is at most
`12*192^(-d)`.

For weighted or typed branching, replace this by an explicit bound that the
probability of two paths remaining unresolved tends to zero.  A uniform
per-state collision bound below one is sufficient.  A deterministic one-child
closed state needs its own terminal graphon and cannot be dismissed as a
measure-zero infinite agreement event.

This infinite-word construction refines **within equal outer classes**.  It
does not justify repeatedly perturbing every fractional off-diagonal block.
The latter requires the martingale construction above or another explicit
consistency argument.

## 4. What H-ROOT-001/002 may currently claim

The positive `latent-precision-002` result supports one coherent global latent
mode and a new 384-class step graphon.  The follow-up derivation in
`recursive-latent.md` now supplies a precise coherent iteration, which can be
audited directly.  On the product space

    {0,...,191} x {+1,-1}^r,

it sets

    W_r((i,s),(j,t))
      = P_ij + C_ij sum_(a=1)^r epsilon_a s_a t_a,

where `C` is the fixed support of all 1248 fractional base blocks.  Sign
averaging kills a monomial unless each positional vertex has even degree in
the perturbation edges for that latent coordinate.  The only nonempty
Eulerian subgraphs of `K4` are its four triangles and three four-cycles.  No
two of these can be edge-disjoint, while each product edge can select only one
latent coordinate.  Therefore no mixed-coordinate term survives and the
complete four-profile has the exact form

    v_r = v_0 + U sum_a epsilon_a^3 + V sum_a epsilon_a^4.

This argument remains valid for repeated base labels because latent signs are
sampled independently at the four graphon positions.  It is an exact typed
profile recurrence, not an untyped 11-state PF recursion.

Feasibility is also exact.  Every sign-product pattern is attainable on an
active pair, so all descendants lie in `[0,1]` if and only if

    sum_a |q_a| <= 14472,             epsilon_a = q_a/65536.

The value 14472 is the minimum probability slack over the active base blocks.
Thus the selected `q=-4752` can be repeated for only three levels
(`3*4752=14256`); a fourth equal level is invalid.  Constant nonzero amplitude
has no infinite fixed point in this family.

An actual infinite refinement is nevertheless valid with absolutely summable
amplitudes and strict total slack.  The series for `W_infinity` then converges
uniformly on
`{0,...,191} x {+1,-1}^N`, giving a symmetric measurable `[0,1]` graphon, and
the finite step graphons converge uniformly to it.  For geometric amplitudes
`epsilon_a=eta*rho^(a-1)`, `|rho|<1`, the exact density is

    F_infinity = F_0
      + alpha eta^3/(1-rho^3)
      + beta  eta^4/(1-rho^4),

with the already derived typed coefficients.  This establishes convergence
and normalization of that specified infinite family.  It does not verify the
separate proposed four-equal-level amplitude candidate or prove a global
optimum over amplitude partitions.

There are therefore two clean next formulations:

- **Composition formulation:** freeze the 384-class cross probabilities and
  recursively replace only its diagonal/equal-class cells by one shared or a
  finite typed family of child graphons.  Derive the 11-state or expanded typed
  profile operator and verify its convergence.
- **Off-diagonal refinement formulation:** construct nested finite partitions,
  choose a feasible zero-mean perturbation in every parent probability cell,
  and verify the bounded-martingale condition.  The additive latent family
  above satisfies this with summable amplitudes and even converges uniformly.
  Density convergence is then automatic, though improvement still requires an
  exact objective calculation.

H-ROOT-002's one-level rank-`r` association-scheme directions require no
recursive theorem: each finite microtype system is another finite step graphon.
For a sequence of growing groups or schemes, a graphon limit is also optional.
If the exact densities `F_k` converge to `F`, then `c4 <= F_k` for every `k`
implies `c4 <= F`.  To claim convergence of the objects themselves, provide a
cut/L1 compactness or character-limit argument and identify the limiting
kernel.

## 5. Seidel-switching no-go audit

The proposed obstruction in `fresh-hypotheses-round2.md` is correct under its
stated scope: `A(x,y)` must be a symmetric `{+1,-1}` kernel and the lifted edge
sign must be exactly

    E((x,s),(y,t)) = A(x,y) s t,

with independent uniform `s,t in {+1,-1}` for sampled vertex types.

For a fixed ordered base quadruple, let `tau_1,...,tau_4` be its four triangle
products.  Their product is one.  A vertex switching can make all six lifted
edges equal exactly when the four triangle products are equal.  In that case
exactly two of the 16 vertex-sign assignments work, so the conditional
monochromatic probability is `1/8`.  Since the number of negative `tau_i` is
even,

    1{tau_1=tau_2=tau_3=tau_4}
      = (tau_1+tau_2+tau_3+tau_4)^2/16
      = 1/4 + (1/4) sum_(three C4s) chi(C4).

After averaging independent base points, the three cycle terms are equal.
For a finite symmetric sign matrix,

    t(C4,A)
      = n^(-4) sum_(i,k) (sum_j A_ij A_jk)^2 >= 0.

For a symmetric measurable sign kernel the same identity is
`||T_A^2||_HS^2 >= 0`.  Therefore the lifted monochromatic density is

    1/32 + (3/32)t(C4,A) >= 1/32.

The normalization is correct: `1/8` is the switching success probability and
the homogeneous-triangle event has density `1/4+(3/4)t(C4,A)`.  Ordered
repeated base labels are included; the algebra uses only `A^2=1` and symmetry,
so they cause no exception.  Ordinary repeated **vertices** again disappear
under finite blow-up realization.

The no-go statement must not be extended to kernels with values strictly
between `-1` and `1`, nonsymmetric kernels, additive perturbations
`P+epsilon st C`, or multi-type association schemes.  The indicator identity
uses `tau_i in {+1,-1}` and the operator-square positivity uses symmetry.

## 6. Proof obligations before a recursive headline

1. State whether the recursion is composition or pair-cell refinement.
2. Give a common probability space or an exact finite profile recurrence.
3. Prove feasibility, symmetry, and normalization at every depth.
4. For profile transfer, prove stochasticity and primitivity/contraction on the
   reachable state; a computed stationary vector alone is insufficient.
5. For graphon refinement, prove conditional mean preservation or an `L1`
   Cauchy bound.
6. Recount at least two finite depths independently and compare them with the
   recurrence before using its fixed point.
7. Keep the one-level 384-class result separate: it is already a valid improved
   asymptotic step graphon and does not depend on any of obligations 2--6 for a
   new recursion.
